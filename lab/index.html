    What grass can be

    Fourteen scenes, drawn live in this window from the same few numbers. Pick one, then walk about in it and change anything.

    Preset

    The species, the style and the field as JSON: copy it to keep it, or paste one here and load it. Pictures brought in from files are not in it, only their names.

    What a frame costs

    The graphics card's own time for the grass and the ground, over five fixed views: its compute passes and its drawing, in milliseconds a frame. A frame at 60 a second has 16.7 ms.
